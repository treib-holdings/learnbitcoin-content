Every live on-chain number on this site - block height, supply,
difficulty, the mempool - comes from a full Bitcoin node, not from
an exchange or a data vendor. The one number here that is not
on-chain is the BTC/USD price, an exchange price passed through
from mempool.space. If the node is lying to you, every other node on Earth
disagrees with it, and you should believe them, not it.

The data feed comes from [ChainQuery.com](https://chainquery.com),
which runs the node and exposes a no-fee public API. Verify, don't
trust.
